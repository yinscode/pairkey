Authorize a session

For supported Solana and EVM native transfers, a session grants bounded permission to a delegate. The grant binds the signing account, destination, recipient, per-operation limit, policy version, and expiration. EVM grants additionally bound gas price and total fees.

Eligible session-only requests do not need a new Solana authorization transaction for each signature. Zero per-signature Solana transaction fees means precisely that: grant creation, revocation, destination execution, and infrastructure still have costs. Production service pricing has not been established.

Both signers still check fresh control state. A policy requiring COMMIT+SESSION needs an exact on-chain approval as well. RedPallas currently requires COMMIT. Total spending budgets, EVM contract calls, and EVM simulation are not implemented.

Solidity integration is planned. Solana remains the sole authorization control plane; an EVM contract cannot currently replace the Solana controller.

Construct a bounded grant

  1. Choose the enrolled account/path and supported destination adapter.
  2. Bind the delegate credential, recipient, per-operation value limit, and validity interval.
  3. For EVM native transfers, bind the enrolled backend-key digest, chain ID, gas-price cap, and total-fee cap.
  4. Publish the grant through your controller program and wait for finalized state.
  5. Attach a proof of possession bound to the exact operation, current policy version, and session when requesting signing.

Both signers check authority before releasing output. Changing a request cannot broaden the registered grant. Policy versions advance by exactly one and invalidate old grants and proofs.

Compose policy requirements

COMMIT, SESSION, access proof, and constrained SIMULATE requirements come from registered policy. A caller cannot choose a weaker mode. When no applicable session replaces exact approval, COMMIT remains required.

The Solana simulation adapter checks a tightly constrained native-transfer operation against a pinned immutable program. EVM SIMULATE and general-purpose destination-state predicates are not implemented.

Revoke or freeze

Revoke the session through your controller program or freeze the namespace. The revocation is permanent for that grant ID; create a new ID for a replacement. Signers refuse future issuance after observing the updated state.

An already issued signature cannot be recalled. Grant limits are per operation, not a cumulative allowance. Do not treat a 0.01 ETH cap as a 0.01 ETH total budget.
