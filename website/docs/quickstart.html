Use your own Solana authority with PairKey’s hosted signers. Register a namespace, choose a chain and path, approve a message, and receive a verifiable signature.

Choose your authority

Solana account →Connect a wallet. Authorize each message directly through the PairKey gateway.Solana program →Enforce your application’s rules, then authorize through a program PDA.

PairKey runs the signers

Both guides use https://pairkey.com/api/v1 and our deployed Solana devnet gateway. You do not need a validator, signing workers, coordinator, or signer secrets. Your wallet pays the devnet fees and account rent for registration and message approvals.

One approval, one signing job

  1. Register: bind a namespace to your wallet or program PDA.
  2. Choose a key: select Solana, EVM, Zcash, NEAR, or Bitcoin and a derivation path.
  3. Approve: authorize the exact message and key on Solana.
  4. Sign: the hosted signers independently check finalized authorization and jointly produce the signature.
  5. Verify: check the signature against the message and public key.

The signature is returned asynchronously. These guides sign messages; they do not broadcast destination transactions.

Choose how permission is enforced

Both quickstarts start with per-message approval. Your wallet approves directly, or your program checks its rules and approves through a PDA. Continue to Policy & rules for custom program logic and account-controlled registered policies.

Current hosted deployment

The service uses Solana devnet, Base Sepolia for the EVM message context, and Bitcoin testnet for the Bitcoin context. Hosted signers currently run in development mode. See hosted service status and limits for the trust model and recovery behavior.
