One authority model, multiple destinations

A Solana controller program governs a namespace and the permissions to use its signing keys. Your application publishes an exact-operation approval or grants a bounded session. Both signers independently check the current authority and participate in producing a signature.

The intended deployment uses two independent TEE signers. Each holds its own share; the signing protocol does not assemble a complete private key. The relay routes work and returns results, while the signers enforce authorization.

The concepts you need

Controller
The authority that governs permissions. In the program-controlled flow, this is your Solana program’s PDA.
Namespace
The registered context that binds derivation authority, public metadata, and the enrolled signer set.
Account path
The application’s identifier for a signing account within that context. Paths are not independent compromise domains.
COMMIT
An exact-operation approval. It binds the approved bytes and allows signing them; destination replay rules determine execution counts.
Session
A scoped grant to a delegate, constrained by account, destination, recipient, per-operation limits, expiry, and policy version.
Attempt and job
An attempt identifies one signing execution. A job tracks submission, progress, and retained results. Neither is a destination transaction.
