05 / TWO WAYS TO AUTHORIZE

Your rules. Two approval paths.

OFF-CHAIN APPROVAL

Zero per-signature Solana transaction fees.

Authorize the exact request with a wallet signature, or evaluate your program's rules through authenticated simulation. No authorization transaction is broadcast.

01 Authorize the exact request
02 Both signers check, then sign
03 Receive and verify the signature

ON-CHAIN APPROVAL

Make every approval part of your program.

Consume an allowance or update application state while approving exact bytes through Solana CPI. Both signers verify the finalized decision.

01 Program commits the decision via CPI
02 Signers verify finalized authority
03 Receive a verified signature

Two paths, chosen per use case. Off-chain approval is the fast, cheap default: the account signs the exact request, or both signers run an authenticated simulation of your program's rules, and no Solana authorization transaction is sent. On-chain approval is for decisions that must consume state, like spending down an allowance: the program records its approval atomically through gateway CPI, and both signers check the finalized result. Fee caveat: zero means no Solana authorization transaction per signature. Policy edits, program commits, RPC or service charges and destination execution are separate costs.
