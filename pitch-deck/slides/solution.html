PAIRKEY

03

PairKey is a programmable key protocol. Three ideas. First, permission is code: a Solana program or account decides who may request a signature and for what exact bytes. Second, signing power is split across two independent signers, each running in its own TEE in the target deployment, each holding its own share and each checking authorization before it participates. Third, the output is an ordinary signature that the destination chain already understands.
