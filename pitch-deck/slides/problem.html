01 / THE PROBLEM

Applications own their logic. They rarely own their signing authority.

CUSTODY

The key sits whole in one place.

Custodial and single-enclave setups protect a complete private key. Security rests on one boundary.

CONTROL

The rules live somewhere else.

Signing policy sits in a vendor's API, or on a different chain from the application it is meant to serve.

REACH

Every chain is a new integration.

EVM, Bitcoin, Zcash and NEAR each bring their own signature scheme, key format and tooling.

PAIRKEY

02

A Solana program can already express rich rules: allowances, roles, time limits. But the moment that application needs to act on another chain, its authority stops at the border. Teams either hand a whole key to a custodian or a single enclave, accept someone else's policy engine, or build signing infrastructure chain by chain. PairKey is built to remove all three compromises.
