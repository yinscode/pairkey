08

Positioning. NEAR Chain Signatures also gives multichain outputs, but authority lives in NEAR accounts and contracts and each request pays NEAR gas and waits for inclusion. Turnkey is fast and off-chain, but authority lives in API credentials and its own policy engine, and the key lives in a signer enclave. PairKey puts authority in the application's own Solana program, splits signing across two independent signers so no full key exists, and lets the app choose between fee-free off-chain approval and atomic on-chain approval. Turnkey publishes a vendor-reported 50 to 100 ms signing figure; we do not yet publish a production latency guarantee, so avoid latency claims in the room.
