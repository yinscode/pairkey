Define signing permissions in a Solana program. Two independent signers enforce them and sign for Solana, EVM, Bitcoin, Zcash and NEAR.

PAIRKEY.COM · INVESTOR PRESENTATION · OCTOBER 2026

PairKey is a programmable key protocol. The one-line version: your application's rules decide who can sign, and two independent signers enforce those rules before producing a signature that works on five chains. The mark itself is the idea: two separate pieces that only form a whole together.
